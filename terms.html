What the app does

Hıfz Takip helps you track your Quran memorization page by page and plan your reviews. The app does not contain the Quran text; pages are numbered according to the 604-page Medina Mushaf. Plans are helpful suggestions: checking your recitation against your own mushaf or with a teacher remains up to you.
