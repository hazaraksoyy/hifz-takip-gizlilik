Hıfz Takip
Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 5, 2026

Hıfz Takip is an app that helps you track your Quran memorization page by page. This page explains which data is kept and where.

Data that stays on your device

Your memorized pages, review ratings, daily plans and settings are stored on your phone, in the app's own database. The app does not send this data to us or to a server of its own; we have no such server.

You do not need an account. The app does not ask for or collect personal information such as your name, email address, location or contacts.

Backups

When you choose "Back up", your data is prepared as a file and your phone's share menu opens. You decide where the file is saved or who it is sent to. The app never sends this file anywhere on its own.

Android automatic backup: If Google backup is on, Android may copy the app's data to your Google account's backup and restore it when you reinstall the app. This copy is in your Google account and does not reach us. You can turn it off or manage it in your phone's Google backup settings.

Purchases

Premium purchases are made through Google Play. Your payment details are processed by Google; the app never sees your card details.

RevenueCat is used to verify your subscription status. It receives the purchase record, a randomly generated anonymous user ID and technical details such as app version, device and operating system type and language; your IP address also reaches RevenueCat when connecting. Your memorization data is not sent to RevenueCat. Details: revenuecat.com/privacy

Notifications

The daily reminder is scheduled locally on your phone. No server is used for notifications.

Ads and analytics

The app has no ads. No usage analytics or tracking tools are used.

If you write to us

If you email us (for example with "Report a translation error"), we receive your email address and what you write. A translation report's subject line includes the app's language and version. We use this only to reply and to fix the problem.

Deleting your data

When you uninstall the app, its data on your phone is deleted. Backup files you saved yourself and the Android backup in your Google account remain under your control; you can delete the Android backup in your phone's or Google account's settings.

Hıfz Takip: data deletion request

Data on your phone: Memorization records, review history, notes and settings do not reach us. The copies on your phone are deleted when you uninstall Hıfz Takip; no request to us is needed. You can delete the Android backup in your Google account yourself.

Purchase records: If you bought Premium, RevenueCat keeps your purchase record and a randomly generated anonymous ID to verify the subscription. To have them deleted:

  1. Send an email with the subject "Hıfz Takip data deletion" to paladinsvideos@gmail.com.
  2. Include the order number (starting with GPA.) from the receipt Google Play sent after your purchase; we use it to find the record.
  3. Your request will be completed within 30 days and you will be told by email.

Deleted: the purchase record and anonymous user ID at RevenueCat. Kept: payment and billing records are held by Google Play for Google's own legal retention periods; these are outside our control. If you have an active subscription after deletion, it continues through Google Play; to reactivate Premium, tap "Restore purchases" in the app.
