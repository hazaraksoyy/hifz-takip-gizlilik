Hıfz Takip
Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 3, 2026

Hıfz Takip is an app that helps you track your Quran memorization page by page. This page explains which data is kept and where.

Data that stays on your device

Your memorized pages, review ratings, daily plans and settings are stored only on your phone, in the app's own database. This data is not sent to us or to any other server.

You do not need an account. Personal information such as your name, email address, location or contacts is neither requested nor collected.

Backups

When you choose "Back up", your data is prepared as a file and your phone's share menu opens. You decide where the file is saved or who it is sent to. The app never sends this file anywhere on its own.

Purchases

Premium purchases are made through Google Play. Your payment details are processed by Google; the app never sees your card details.

RevenueCat is used to verify your subscription status. It receives the purchase record and a randomly generated anonymous user ID. Your memorization data is not sent to RevenueCat. Details: revenuecat.com/privacy

Notifications

The daily reminder is scheduled locally on your phone. No server is used for notifications.

Ads and analytics

The app has no ads. No usage analytics or tracking tools are used.

Deleting your data

When you uninstall the app, all of its data on your phone is deleted. Any backup files you saved yourself remain under your control.

Hıfz Takip: data deletion request

Data on your phone: Memorization records, review history, notes and settings exist only on your phone. They are permanently deleted when you uninstall Hıfz Takip; no request is needed.

Purchase records: If you bought Premium, RevenueCat keeps your purchase record and a randomly generated anonymous ID to verify the subscription. To have them deleted:

  1. Send an email with the subject "Hıfz Takip data deletion" to paladinsvideos@gmail.com.
  2. Include the order number (starting with GPA.) from the receipt Google Play sent after your purchase; we use it to find the record.
  3. Your request will be completed within 30 days and you will be told by email.

Deleted: the purchase record and anonymous user ID at RevenueCat. Kept: payment and billing records are held by Google Play for Google's own legal retention periods; these are outside our control. If you have an active subscription after deletion, it continues through Google Play; to reactivate Premium, tap "Restore purchases" in the app.
